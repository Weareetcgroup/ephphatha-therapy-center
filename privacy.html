Privacy

Simple privacy, by design.

This starter website intentionally avoids paid analytics, advertising trackers and a server-side patient database.

Appointment form

The appointment form runs in your browser. When submitted, it creates a WhatsApp message containing the details you entered. The website itself does not store the form submission on a server.

Contact information

If you contact Ephphatha by phone, WhatsApp or email, those communications are handled through the relevant service and the center's normal communication process.

Cookies and analytics

This starter version does not intentionally set advertising cookies or use paid analytics. If analytics, embedded social feeds, online payments or a patient portal are added later, this notice should be updated accordingly.

Children's information

Please avoid sending highly sensitive health records through the general appointment form. Clinical records should be shared only through a method approved by the center.

External services

Links to WhatsApp, Google Maps and Instagram take you to third-party services with their own privacy practices.
