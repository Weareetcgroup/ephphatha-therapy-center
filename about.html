About Ephphatha

Care that listens before it plans.

Ephphatha Therapy Center was established in Chennai in 2020 with a simple purpose: create a supportive place where communication, development, learning and independence can be worked on together — without losing sight of the person behind the goals.

DN
Diana Nixon

Founder & Clinical Lead · Senior SLP & Audiologist

Founder-led care

A multidisciplinary center with communication at its heart.

Ephphatha brings together speech and language therapy, occupational therapy, early intervention, behavioural support, special education, sensory-based support and selected adult communication services.

What we want families to feel

Clear. Supported. Involved.

Clear

You should understand why a goal matters, what is being worked on and what the next step is.

Supported

Questions are welcome. Therapy is a partnership, not a process families are expected to navigate alone.

Involved

Useful strategies can be carried into everyday routines so skills have opportunities to grow beyond the therapy room.

Respected

Every person has a different profile, pace, strengths and priorities. Care should reflect that.

Our location

Local care in the Gowrivakkam / Sembakkam area.

We serve families from nearby Chennai neighborhoods and can discuss online consultation or selected online services when suitable.
