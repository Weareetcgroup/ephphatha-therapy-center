How it works

A clearer first step for parents.

Families should know what happens next. Our website makes the journey easy to understand before you even call.

Tell us your concern

Share what you are noticing, the age, and what you want help with.

Assessment / consultation

The clinician understands strengths, needs and whether formal assessment is appropriate.

Personalized care plan

Goals, session format and recommended frequency are discussed with the family.

Review progress

Therapy evolves as skills grow, priorities change and new goals become meaningful.
