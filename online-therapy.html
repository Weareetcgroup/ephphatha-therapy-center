Care beyond the clinic

Online support when it is clinically appropriate and practical.

Online care can reduce travel and make caregiver coaching easier, but it is not the right format for every goal. Ephphatha can help decide whether an online consultation or selected online therapy service suits the individual.

How online support can help

Simple technology. Real interaction.

✓
Initial discussions and parent / caregiver guidance
✓
Selected speech and communication goals
✓
Home-program review and strategy coaching
✓
Follow-up sessions when a clinician confirms online delivery is suitable
Some sensory-motor, hands-on or complex assessments may be better suited to in-person care. The center will guide you based on the specific need.
What you need

No special platform or paid app required.

A phone, tablet or laptop

A larger screen can be helpful for activities, but the clinician will guide you.

Stable internet

A quiet, well-lit space and reliable connection make sessions smoother.

Caregiver participation

For young children, an adult may need to help with materials, routines or practice.

Everyday materials

When possible, therapy can use familiar toys or household items already available at home.
