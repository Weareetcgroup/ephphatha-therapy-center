Multidisciplinary support

Therapy should fit the person — not the other way around.

Our services are organized around communication, sensory-motor development, learning, participation and independence. A clinician can help decide which service or combination is appropriate after understanding the concern.

Speech & Language Therapy

Assessment-led intervention for communication needs across childhood and selected adult presentations.

  • Speech sound clarity and articulation
  • Expressive and receptive language
  • Fluency / stammering
  • Social communication and functional communication
  • Voice and other communication needs where appropriate
  • Caregiver guidance and home practice strategies
Ask about speech therapy →

Occupational Therapy

Support for skills that help children participate more comfortably and independently in everyday life.

  • Fine-motor and hand skills
  • Sensory processing and regulation
  • Play and participation
  • Attention and task engagement
  • Self-care and daily living skills
  • Coordination and functional motor planning
Ask about occupational therapy →

Behavioural Support

Compassionate, practical support focused on participation, routines, communication and meaningful daily functioning.

  • Understanding triggers and patterns
  • Building useful routines
  • Supporting emotional regulation
  • Increasing participation in daily activities
  • Parent / caregiver strategy coaching
Discuss behavioural concerns →

Early Intervention

Development-focused support for young children when communication, play, movement, learning or participation needs attention.

  • Early communication and interaction
  • Play and engagement
  • Sensory-motor development
  • School-readiness foundations
  • Family coaching for everyday routines
Ask about early intervention →

Special Education

Individualized learning support designed to build confidence, access and functional academic skills.

  • Pre-academic and foundational learning
  • Reading, writing and numeracy support
  • Attention and learning routines
  • Visual supports and structured teaching
  • School-readiness and functional education
Ask about learning support →

Sensory Integration Support

Therapy activities may use movement, body awareness and sensory experiences to support regulation and participation when clinically indicated.

  • Sensory regulation
  • Body awareness
  • Balance and coordination
  • Motor planning
  • Participation in home and school routines
Discuss sensory concerns →

Auditory-Verbal Therapy

Listening and spoken-language support for appropriate candidates using hearing technology, with strong caregiver participation.

  • Listening skill development
  • Spoken-language goals
  • Parent coaching
  • Home carry-over strategies
  • Coordination with hearing-related care where needed
Ask about AVT →

Adult Communication Support

Selected services for adults with speech, language, fluency, voice or acquired communication concerns.

  • Communication assessment
  • Speech clarity and fluency
  • Language support after acquired changes where appropriate
  • Functional communication goals
  • Home strategies and caregiver guidance
